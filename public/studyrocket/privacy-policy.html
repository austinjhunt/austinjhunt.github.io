StudyRocket privacy policy

A plain-English summary of what data StudyRocket collects, what it does with it, and the controls you have over it.

Last updated: 27 July 2026

The short version

StudyRocket turns a topic, a Wikipedia article, or a PDF you upload into a practice quiz. To do that, the app needs to:

  • Know who you are (sign-in), so your quizzes are tied to your account.
  • Send your topic, article, or PDF to a cloud AI service so it can write questions from it.
  • Count how many quizzes and questions you generate, so plan limits can be enforced.

We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps. You can delete your account and everything attached to it from inside the app at any time.

One thing worth reading properly: anything you send for quiz generation — including PDFs — is processed by a third-party cloud AI service. See Section 4 before uploading anything sensitive.

3. Permissions the app requests

Files and documents
Requested only when you tap to choose a PDF. Both iOS and Android use the system document picker, which means the app never gets access to your file system — only the single file you select.

StudyRocket does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo library access.

4. How AI generation works, and what it means for your files

When you generate a quiz, the source material — your topic, an excerpt of the Wikipedia article, or the PDF itself — is sent from your device to our server, and from there to Google's Gemini model, which writes the questions. The same happens when an open-ended answer is graded.

We do not store the PDF or its text. The file is held only for the duration of that request. Afterwards we keep only the file name and its size, so you can recognise which quiz came from which document.

What happens on the AI provider's side is not fully within our control. Content you send for quiz generation is processed by a third-party cloud AI service, and depending on that service's configuration and terms it may be retained for a period, reviewed for quality or safety, or used to train future models.

Treat anything you upload as leaving your control. Do not upload confidential, proprietary, or sensitive documents, material containing other people's personal information, or anything you are not permitted to share. StudyRocket is intended for your own study material.

5. Third-party services we use

StudyRocket relies on the following services to function. Each link is to that service's own privacy policy.

  • Firebase Authentication — manages your sign-in. Firebase privacy
  • Sign in with Apple — when you choose that sign-in option. Apple privacy
  • Google Sign-In — when you choose that sign-in option. Google privacy
  • Google Gemini (via Vertex AI) — generates your questions and grades open-ended answers. See Section 4. Google Cloud terms
  • Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions — store your account and quizzes, and run generation server-side.
  • Firebase App Check (App Attest on iOS, Play Integrity on Android) — verifies your app installation is genuine.
  • Wikipedia — article search and content. Fetched by our server, not by your device, so Wikipedia never receives your IP address or learns what you looked up. Wikimedia privacy

6. The device identifier

Generating a quiz costs us money on every request, so the app applies a daily limit per device alongside the monthly limit per account. To do that we take the identifier your operating system gives apps from this publisher — identifierForVendor on iOS, ANDROID_ID on Android — and store a one-way SHA-256 hash of it with a date and a count.

That record holds nothing else. It is not linked to your account, and it cannot be reversed to the original identifier or traced back to you.

It is deliberately not deleted when you delete your account — see Section 7.

7. Data retention and deletion

What stays in the cloud

StudyRocket stores your quizzes on our servers rather than only on your phone, so they follow you between devices. Your authentication record (email, display name, UID) sits in Firebase Authentication; your quizzes, questions, answers, and usage counters sit in Cloud Firestore. All of it persists until you delete your account.

What is not retained

The contents of PDFs you upload are not kept past the request that generated the quiz — though see Section 4 regarding the AI service that processes them.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Account → Delete account. You will be asked to sign in once more to confirm it is really you. This deletes:

  • Your Firebase Authentication record (email, display name, UID).
  • Every quiz and question you have generated.
  • Your answers to open-ended questions, and their grades.
  • Your monthly usage counters.
  • Any share links you created — links you have already sent to other people will stop working.

The device usage counter (deviceUsage/<hash>) is not deleted. It is keyed to your device rather than your account and cannot be traced back to you — it holds only a date and a number. We retain it so the daily limit cannot be reset simply by deleting and recreating an account, and it is overwritten automatically each day. The underlying identifier is reset by your operating system when you uninstall the app.

Account deletion is immediate and irreversible. We cannot recover deleted data. If you no longer have the app installed, see Delete your account for how to request deletion by email.

Server logs may retain request records for a short period under Google Cloud's default retention, for security and abuse investigation.

8. Children

StudyRocket is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. International users

Our backend infrastructure is hosted by Google Cloud, primarily in the United States. By using StudyRocket you understand that your data may be processed in the U.S. and in other countries where Google operates.

10. Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you can at any time:

  • Sign out, from the account screen.
  • Delete your account and everything attached to it (Account → Delete account).
  • Email austinjhunt@studyrocket.dev to request a copy of the data tied to your account, or to ask us to correct it.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights to access, export, or object to processing. We will respond to any such request within 30 days. We do not sell personal information as that term is defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act.

11. Security

All traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit with HTTPS/TLS. Access to data in Firestore is governed by security rules that confine each account to its own data, every request to our backend is verified with Firebase App Check and your signed-in credentials, and the parts of the system that cost money or grant entitlements can only be written server-side.

That said, no system is perfectly secure. Enable two-factor authentication on the Apple or Google account you sign in with.
