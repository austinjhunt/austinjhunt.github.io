Privacy policy

Last updated 27 July 2026

StudyRocket is operated by Study Rocket, LLC, a South Carolina limited liability company. This policy describes what the StudyRocket mobile app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to get rid of it.

What we collect

Data Why
Email address and display name Supplied by Apple or Google when you sign in. Used to identify your account and show you your own quizzes. We never receive your password.
Quiz content The topics you type, the Wikipedia articles you choose, and the questions generated for you. Stored so your quizzes are there when you come back.
Answers to open-ended questions Sent to an AI model for grading, and stored with the score and feedback so you can review them.
PDF file name and size Recorded so you can recognise which quiz came from which document. The contents of the PDF are not stored — see below.
Usage counts How many quizzes and questions you have generated this month, to enforce your plan's limits.
A one-way hash of a device identifier Used only to apply fair-use limits per device. See Device identifiers.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or browsing activity. StudyRocket contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

What happens to PDFs you upload

When you generate a quiz from a PDF, the file is sent from your device to our server, and from there to Google's Gemini model so that questions can be written from it. The file is held only for the duration of that request. We do not store the file, and we do not keep its text. All we retain afterwards is the file name and its size.

Data sent to Gemini is processed by Google Cloud under the Google Cloud Platform terms. Please do not upload documents containing other people's personal information, or material you are not permitted to share.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. It is shared only with the services needed to run the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions) — hosts your account and your quizzes.
  • Google Vertex AI (Gemini) — receives the topic, article excerpt, or PDF you are generating from, and any open-ended answer being graded.
  • Apple and Google — handle sign-in. They tell us your email address and name; we tell them nothing about you.

Wikipedia articles are fetched by our server rather than by your device, so Wikipedia does not receive your IP address or learn what you looked up.

Device identifiers

Generating a quiz costs us money on every request, so the app applies a daily limit per device as well as a monthly limit per account. To do that we take the identifier your operating system gives apps from this publisher — identifierForVendor on iOS, ANDROID_ID on Android — and store a one-way SHA-256 hash of it alongside a date and a count.

This record contains nothing else. It is not linked to your account, and it cannot be turned back into the original identifier or traced to you.

It is deliberately not deleted when you delete your account. If it were, the daily limit could be reset simply by deleting and recreating an account, which would leave the service open to abuse at our expense. The underlying identifier is reset by your operating system when you uninstall the app.

How long we keep things

  • Your account, quizzes, and answers: until you delete your account.
  • PDF contents: not retained past the request that generated the quiz.
  • Hashed device records: rolling, and reset by uninstalling the app.
  • Server logs: retained by Google Cloud's default retention for operational and abuse-investigation purposes.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time — Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, every quiz and question you have generated, your answers, your usage history, and any share links you created. It cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the app installed, see Delete your account for how to request deletion by email.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to its processing. The in-app deletion above satisfies the deletion right immediately. For anything else, email austinjhunt@studyrocket.dev and we will respond within 30 days.

We do not sell personal information as that term is defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act.

Children

StudyRocket is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and the app will tell you the next time you open it.
