2. What the App does

StudyRocket takes a topic you type, a Wikipedia article you choose, or a PDF you upload, sends it to Google's Gemini model, and generates a practice quiz — multiple choice, true/false, fill in the blanks, and open-ended questions. Open-ended answers you write are sent to the same model for a score and written feedback.

It is a study aid. It is not a tutor, an examiner, or a source of authoritative information.
