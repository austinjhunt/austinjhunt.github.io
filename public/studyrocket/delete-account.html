StudyRocket Privacy Terms

Delete your account

Last updated 27 July 2026

You can delete your StudyRocket account and everything attached to it at any time. There are two ways, depending on whether you still have the app installed.

If you have the app

This is immediate and does not require contacting us.

  1. Open StudyRocket.
  2. Tap Account in the top right.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. You will be asked to sign in with Apple or Google once more — this is to prove it is really you before anything is deleted.

If you no longer have the app

Email austinjhunt@studyrocket.dev from the email address on the account, with the subject Account deletion request.

We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner. We may need to verify that you control the address before deleting anything.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — email address and display name.
  • Every quiz and question you have generated.
  • Your answers to open-ended questions, and their grades.
  • Your usage history.
  • Any share links you created. Links you have already sent to other people will stop working.

This is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is not deleted

One record is kept: a one-way hash of a device identifier, stored with a date and a count of quizzes generated that day. It is used to apply a daily fair-use limit per device.

It contains no other information, is not linked to your account, and cannot be traced back to you. It is kept because deleting it would allow the daily limit to be reset by deleting and recreating an account, which would leave the service open to abuse at our expense. The underlying identifier is reset by your phone when you uninstall the app.

Server logs may retain request records for a short period under Google Cloud's default retention, for security and abuse investigation.

There is more detail in the privacy policy.

Deleting your StudyRocket account does not affect your Apple or Google account. It only removes the StudyRocket account that was created when you signed in.
